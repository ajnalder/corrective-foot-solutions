Our approach

A different kind of podiatry, built around your feet

Foot Mobilisation Therapy (FMT) is a hands‑on technique that gently restores movement to stiff, misaligned or dysfunctional joints, encouraging the foot back through its natural range of motion.

  • Stimulates natural repair. Your body does the healing; we give it the alignment to do it.
  • Keeps working after you leave. Orthotics only work while you wear them. FMT changes the structure of your foot.
  • Keep your life moving. You’re encouraged to continue normal activity throughout treatment.

Why FMT

Orthotics manage the problem. We correct it.

A quick look at how our approach differs from traditional podiatry.

Traditional orthotics

Insoles that support the foot in its current shape
  • ✕Only works while you wear them
  • ✕Limits the shoes you can wear
  • ✕Treats the symptom, not the joint restriction
  • ✕Often needs replacing and refitting
  • ✕Surgery is the usual “next step”

Foot Mobilisation Therapy

Hands‑on correction of the joints causing the pain
  • ✓Improvement continues between and after visits
  • ✓Wear the footwear that suits you
  • ✓Addresses the underlying cause
  • ✓Gentle, painless and minimal risk
  • ✓A real alternative to foot surgery

How it works

Three steps to walking comfortably again

Every plan starts with a thorough assessment, so you know exactly what’s going on and what to expect, before any treatment begins.

01

Assess

A detailed hands‑on assessment of every joint in your foot and ankle, plus how you stand, walk and move. You’ll see objective measurements, not guesswork.

02

Mobilise

Gentle, targeted mobilisation of the restricted joints. Most people describe it as a firm, comfortable stretch. No needles, no cutting, no bulky insoles.

03

Strengthen

Simple exercises from your client portal keep the correction and build strength, so your feet hold their new alignment for the long term.
